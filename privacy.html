Privacy policy

shorts-bot is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It requests permission to upload videos and read the owner's own channel and video details.
  • The login token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared, or sent anywhere except YouTube.
  • No data from other people is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
